# Findings: 08 current measurement  (reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-11-24, commit 3e1e31e)

## Summary
The schematic side of all four chains is right and matches 5.1 in topology, sign, J2 pins and values. The PFC and DAB chains are ready to order. The DAB chain is better than 5.1: the Kelvin via sits in the R83 pad, and the shunt-to-reference distance is 15.6 mm instead of 36 mm.

**The heater chain (R30 → U1A) lost its Kelvin take-off when Q10 became an SMD part 12 mm away.** The In2 sense line now starts at the Q10 source via (−12.8, −68.6). Between that via and R30 lies 12.5 mm of 0.8 mm B.Cu, about 6–8 mΩ, which carries the whole Q10 switch current. HEATER_I therefore reads mostly switch current through copper (with copper TCR), not the input current through the 4 mΩ shunt. This is **should fix**. It is a single In2 track and via, and the corridor is free.

No blockers for ordering in this part. Owner input still open: the shunt value is not decided. The fab BOM fits none of R21 / R30 / R83 / R93 ("consign 4 mΩ or accept 5 mΩ").

Note on the checklist: the board numbers match the checklist except as noted. Stages 13a–13d did not touch this part. Two checklist hints are wrong. R83's "1.2 mm DHB_CURRENT on In2" is the C67/C70 HF return, not part of the sense path. C87 also stood next to U1 on 5.1, not only on 5.2.

## Findings (ranked, most severe first)

### F1 HEATER_I sense line taps at Q10, not at R30 – 12.5 mm of power track inside the measurement
- Severity: **should fix** (the board works, but the heater current channel is worse than 5.1 and no longer measures what the firmware expects)
- What is wrong: the HEATER_CURRENT Kelvin line (In2 0.2 mm → R106) starts at the Q10 source via (−12.8, −68.6). Q10.3 sits at (−16.0, −68.1) since the stage-3 Q10/Q11 swap; R30 stays at (−11.2, −80.4). In between runs a 0.8 mm × 12.46 mm B.Cu trunk (1 oz) plus half a via. That is 7.7 mΩ at 35 µm, 6.0 mΩ at 45 µm, about +0.4 mΩ via, i.e. **1.5–2× the 4 mΩ shunt**. The trunk carries the whole Q10 source current, because C20 / C22 / C41 return at R30 pad 1. The amplifier therefore sees V ≈ I_Q10·(6–8 mΩ) + I_R30·4 mΩ. Effects:
  - DC gain is ≈ 2.5–3× too high.
  - About 2/3 of the signal has a copper TCR of 0.39 %/K, so ≈ 0.26 %/K drift. Firmware gain calibration cannot remove that.
  - The waveform is now the Q10 switch current, half-waves at the LLC frequency passed by the 342 kHz bandwidth, not the bulk input current.
  - About 4 nH of track inductance (vs the 0.7 nH the 5.6p compensation assumes) adds L·di/dt.
  - At cold start (≈ 1.4 A Q10 peak) the positive excursion reaches ≈ 1.1–1.4 V. With up to 0.7 V of Vos at gain 100 (D1), that clips against the 1.6 V half-range.

  In 5.1 Q10.3 stood next to R30.1 and the tap via (−11.2, −79.1) was at the pad.
- Evidence: `python3` track dump of HEATER_CURRENT from `/tmp/tube_psu_review_1000/b52.json`: In2 (−3.8, −68.3)→(−12.6, −68.3) and on to U1; B.Cu w 0.8: (−12.8, −68.6)→(−12.8, −71.0)→(−11.2, −72.6)→(−11.2, −80.4). `rv bq pads HEATER_CURRENT` gives Q10.3 at (−16.017, −68.112). `rv bq51 net HEATER_CURRENT` gives the 5.1 via at (−11.2, −79.1). README l 1371 ("Q10.3 → via (−12.8, −68.6) … → B.Cu under Q11 → R30.1") and l 2033 (the In2 stub at R30 pad 1 deleted as "joined to nothing"). Resistance ρL/(w·t) = 1.72e−8 × 12.46e−3 / (0.8e−3 × 35e−6) = 7.65 mΩ. Checklist 11 B3 traced the same power path but read the In2 piece as a separate sense line.
- Smallest fix: add a 0.5/0.3 via in R30 pad 1 at its north end (≈ (−11.2, −79.5), the 5.1 place). Run In2 0.2 mm north along x ≈ −11.4 to y −68.3 and join the existing lane there. Cut the ≈ 1.2 mm of lane that touches the trunk via (−12.8, −68.6), keeping the DRC clearance to it. The In2 box x −13.5 … −10, y −80.5 … −68 holds only this lane, the trunk via and one GND via at (−10.05, −69.07). The fix also removes the 0.268 mm HEATER_LOW_GATE neighbour.
- Confidence: high on the geometry and the current path. Medium on the size of the effect (outer copper thickness after plating, actual Q10 current shape); a bench capture of HEATER_I against a current probe would settle it.

## Compared with 5.1
- **Better:**
  - Common-mode and output range. The LMH6658 at 3.3 V was outside its guaranteed CMVR (V+ − 2.0 V ≈ 1.3 V < 1.57–1.63 V inputs) and swung only to ≈ 0.8 V from the rails. The LMH6643 has V+ − 1.2/1.4 V and 25–75 mV.
  - Balanced ESL compensation on all four channels.
  - Local 100 nF at U1.
  - DAB Kelvin via in the R83 pad.
  - DAB shunt ↔ reference distance 15.6 mm (5.1 36.3 mm).
  - Lower IB.
- **Equal:**
  - Topology, sign, J2 assignment logic and the PFC layout (taps, reference distance, U4 group and C76 decoupling identical).
  - Output-line routing and crosstalk exposure, including C11-Pad1 ↔ PRI_DHB_HI 21.8 mm on In2.
  - Shunt and op-amp footprints.
- **Worse:**
  - **HEATER_I Kelvin take-off (F1).**
  - Heater shunt ↔ reference 26.4 mm vs 20.0 mm (within 1.5×).
  - Bandwidth 825 vs 993 kHz (PFC) and slower mux recovery. Both are covered by the new dwell rule (NR 4c.4).
  - TCVos 5 vs 2 µV/K typ (F5).
