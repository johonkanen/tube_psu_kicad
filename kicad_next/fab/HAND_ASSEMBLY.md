24 parts: C19 C28 C29 C30 C31 C32 C37 C38 C56 C64 C65 C66 C81 C82 C84 C85 C86 C102 C117 CM1 CM4 J2 J4 RL1 (README "Through-hole parts in the BOM"). If they are unticked at the order, fit them after group 3 here. Orientation as `ROTATION_CHECK.md` (electrolytics: + on the square pad). Notes:

* **J2** (XKB X1321FV-2x25-C43D24, female 2 x 25, 1.27 mm): check mating height (4.3 mm insulator + card pin length) and orientation / pin 1 against the control card's male right-angle header before soldering.
* **J4** (UART 1 x 6, 2.54 mm): 1 ISO_UART_GND, 2 TXD in, 3 ISO_UART_GND, 4 RXD out, 5 ISO_UART_GND, 6 UART_3V3 in. Pin 6 must be the far end's 3.3 V I/O supply and must always be connected (findings 04 F6).
* **C102** (56 µF 250 V): + on ISO_GND, - on -100V BIAS (negative rail): the square pad is + as on every electrolytic.
