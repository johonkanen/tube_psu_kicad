**The board has no silkscreen** (on purpose): there is no pin-1 dot, polarity mark or refdes on the board, and JLC's engineers cannot compare against a legend. Judge pin 1 / cathode / + in the preview from the pad pattern (copper) and the position given here. The rotations in the CPL use the corrections of `ROTATION_FIX` (README "Rotations"), which come from the common KiCad -> JLC tools and are **not verified in JLC's preview**.

How to read the table:

* Directions are board directions in the **top view** (as the Gerbers are drawn: north = up = +Y of the CPL, east = right = +X). For bottom-side parts the top view looks *through* the board; the last column gives the same direction **seen from below** (east and west swapped), which is how a bottom view normally shows it - check which of the two JLC's bottom preview uses with one part first (e.g. the first bottom SOIC).
* The marker is the pad that fixes the orientation: IC pin 1 (dot / bevel on the package), diode cathode (band), electrolytic + (on SMD cans the cut corners of the plastic base are on the + side, the dark mark on the top is -), LFPAK / DPAK gate pin and tab. The offset is from the centre of the part's pad pattern, in mm (x / y).
* `CPL rot` is what JLC receives; `KiCad rot` the board orientation; `Corr.` the `ROTATION_FIX` value.
* Proteus-numbered footprints (`*_Proteus`): the pad names differ from the data-sheet pin numbers on SOT-23_Proteus (JEDEC pin 1 = pad 2, the single pad = pad 1); the position given is the physical pin 1.
