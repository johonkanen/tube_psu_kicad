### Transcription rules (for the sheet sessions)

- Use only `tube_psu:` symbols, exactly the `lib_id` of `lib/part_map.csv` for each refdes,
  and that file's value. Refdes = the `refdes` column (KiCad names: `P15V_OUT`, `P5V_OUT`,
  `N5V_OUT`, `N80V_OUT`, `SEC_GND`, `P400V` for the Proteus terminals `+15V OUT`, `+5V OUT`,
  `-5V OUT`, `-80V OUT`, `SEC GND`, `+400V`).
- **Net names = IPC names** (`reference/pins_by_net.md`), whatever the PDF shows:
  - case: the PDF often writes lowercase (`iso_GND`, `5v_isol`, `20V_ISO_sec`, `gate1`,
    `Cuk_cap_meas2`, `DHB_HI_gate_sec`, `res_in`, …) – use the IPC spelling (`ISO_GND`,
    `5V_ISOL`, `20V_ISO_SEC`, `GATE1`, `CUK_CAP_MEAS2`, `DHB_HI_GATE_SEC`, `RES_IN`);

### Symbol changes (tools/gen_symbols.py, reasons)

- **SI8274 pin 2 `NC1`: type no_connect → passive.** The Proteus design wires a signal to it
  (DRV5-2 = PRI_DHB_HI, DRV6-2 = ISO_SEC_DHB_LO; the PDF shows the wires). KiCad drops a
  no_connect-type pin from the netlist (`unconnected-(DRV5-NC1-Pad2)`), so with the original type
  the pin could not be connected and compare reported the nets split. On the real Si8274 pin 2 is
  NC (the single PWM input on pin 1 drives both outputs with the R40/R25 dead time) – the extra
  signal is harmless but a design oddity worth knowing. Pins 7/12/13 stay no_connect (N/C in IPC).
- **SI8661 pins 6 / 11: `VO_E` out / `VI_E` in → `VI_E` input / `VO_E` output** (2026-10-22, before
  sheet 3). The hand-typed table in gen_symbols.py had channel E reversed. The PDF shows 6 = VI_E,
  11 = VO_E; the DSN device SI8661 has two pinouts, `SO16` (VI_E = 11, VO_E = 6 – wrong) and `SO16W`
  (VI_E = 6, VO_E = 11) and the drawn symbol ($PINDEFAULT records) and the layout use SO16W; the nets
  agree (MCU clock J2 → ISO-6, ISO-11 → ADC CLK, both channels). The real Si8661 is 5 forward (A–E)
  / 1 reverse (F); description corrected. Pin numbers/nets unchanged, only names and ERC types.
- **SCF20-05-550 (common-mode chokes CM1 / CM4) now derived from Device:Filter_EMI_LL_1423** instead
  of Device:Transformer_1P_1S (2026-10-22, sheet 5). The transformer shape has both ends of a winding
  on one side, so a left-to-right mains line through the choke cannot be drawn without a wire
  crossing; the EMI-filter shape is a pass-through (as the PDF draws it, and as ac_psu's chokes).
  Proteus ids mapped to the PDF positions: 1 top-left, 2 top-right (winding 1-2), 3 bottom-left,
  4 bottom-right (winding 3-4); pin numbers / nets unchanged (`pins={"2": "1", "3": "2", "1": "3",
  "4": "4"}`).
- **ETD34_TRAN_1P1S pins 3 / 4 swapped in position** (2026-10-22, sheet 7): the KiCad source symbol
  (Device:Transformer_1P_1S) has its pin 3 at the bottom right and pin 4 at the top right; Proteus draws
  TR3 with pin 3 (TRANS_SEC) top right and 4 (DHB_HI_SOURCE_SEC) bottom right, like pins 1 (top) / 2
  (bottom) on the primary. The symbol has no polarity dots, so only the pin positions changed
  (`pins={"3": "4", "4": "3"}` in gen_symbols.py); numbers, names and nets are unchanged. Without the
  swap the secondary wires of sheet 7 would cross or the layout would be mirrored against the PDF.
- **IXDD609 pin 6 `OUTB`: output → passive.** OUTA and OUTB are the same driver output on two
  pins and are tied together on sheet 9 (both → R1/R14 resp. R19/R20); two output-type pins on
  one net is an ERC error. IXDD604 (DRV4, sheet 6) has separate outputs and is unchanged.

### Pinout findings checked against the pads (confirmed / refuted)

The test: fit the *unmoved* KiCad (JEDEC) footprint with the Proteus renumbering onto the IPC pads;
a small residual means the Proteus ids sit on the JEDEC pads the renumbering says.

- **REF1 (ISL21010 on the ADR380 device, Proteus 1=GND 2=VIN 3=VOUT) – board correct.** On the
  bottom-side pads the plain KiCad SOT-23 fits only with Proteus 1 = JEDEC pin 3, 2 = pin 1, 3 =
  pin 2 (0.020 mm; the other chirality 2 = pin 2, 3 = pin 1 is 1.89 mm off). ISL21010 / ADR380:
  pin 1 = VIN, 2 = VOUT, 3 = GND → VIN, VOUT and GND are physically on the right pads; Proteus only
  numbers the SOT-23 pads differently (same as ac_psu REF3025).
- **Q9 (2N7000 value on the BSS138 device, Proteus 1=D 2=G 3=S) – correct.** Same package and
  mapping: D on JEDEC 3, G on 1, S on 2 = the standard SOT-23 MOSFET pinout.
- **Q1/Q2 (BUK9Y15-60E LFPAK56, Proteus 1=S×3 2=G 3=D) – correct.** The KiCad LFPAK56 (pins 1–3 S,
  4 G, tab D) renumbered 1,2,3→1, 4→2, 5→3 fits the top-side pads with 0.52 mm before moving
  (tab offset only); gate at the other end would be 3.8 mm off. Gate = pin 4 position, as the
  datasheet.
- **TBS22K (DB1):** pads 1 (AC2) and 3 (AC1) on one side, 2 (DC+) and 4 (GND) on the other; the
  symbol's AC1/AC2 names are swapped against the nets (Phase 1 finding) – both AC inputs, nothing
  to do in the footprint.
- **SICD1/SICD2 (C3D02060A TO-220-2):** the two-pad pattern is symmetric, so the geometry cannot
  tell which hole is pin 1; the footprint takes pin 1 = A, pin 2 (tab) = K (datasheet order); the
  nets come from the IPC either way. Only the pin-1 mark in the silk depends on it.
- **TR2 centre taps:** no pads in the IPC (Phase 1 finding stands); the EF25 footprint has the eight
  pads 1,2,4,5,6,7,9,10.
- **C102 (UCS2G560MND9) is not on a snap-in footprint**: Proteus package "10MM ELKO 5MM SPACING"
  (radial, 5 mm pitch, 0.812 drill, like C19/C30). A 400 V 56 µF UCS is ≥ 12.5 mm in diameter, so
  the printed outline is probably too small for the part (the 5 mm lead spacing matches a 12.5 mm

- **C82** has two different drills in Proteus (pin 1 0.812, pin 2 1.016; the other four DEBB caps
