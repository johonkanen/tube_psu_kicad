# Next revision (tube_power 5.2) – implementation plan

Status 2026-11-17, plan only (nothing of it is built yet). **What** changes is in
[NEXT_REVISION.md](NEXT_REVISION.md) (items referenced as NR x.y); this file is **how** and in
which order. User decisions so far: **6-layer board**, **Fable does the parts placement**, the
changes of NEXT_REVISION.md, JLC assembly (part numbers: `lib/jlc_parts.csv`, being filled).

## 0. Principles

- The migration (`kicad/`, `tools/check_all.sh`) stays frozen as the verified 5.1 baseline. The new
  revision lives next to it in **`kicad_next/`** (project `tube_psu_5_2`), so the 5.1 checks keep
  running unchanged.
- The 5.1 schematic is generated by scripts (`tools/sheets/*.py`, `tools/gen_symbols.py`). For 5.2
  the scripts are **forked** to `tools/next/` and edited there: every schematic change is a reviewable
  script diff, regenerated on demand. Once the schematic is frozen, the generated `.kicad_sch` become
  the source (scripts retired) – decided at the end of phase 2.
- The board is laid out by hand / by agents in KiCad (no more Proteus gerber import). Every phase ends
  with ERC / DRC clean and a short entry in `STATUS.md`.
- One phase per session; each phase leaves a `next_prompt.md` for the next one (as in the migration).

Isolation domains and barriers (fill the spacing column from IEC 62368-1 for the final working
voltages, PD2, material group IIIa; inner layers count as solid insulation):

| Domain | Nets | Barrier to primary | Spacing target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | GND (mains-referenced), DC+ 400 V, AC1/AC2, 15V, 5V, +3V3 | – | functional between nets by voltage |
| DAB secondary | ISO_GND, 420V, 15V_ISO_SEC, 5V_ISOL, −80 V | TR3, TR1, ISO2 | **reinforced** (D6) |
| Heater | HT_GND, 6V3 | TR2, ISO1 | **reinforced** (D6) |
| UART island (new) | ISO_UART_GND, cable 5 V / 3.3 V | Si8621BD | **reinforced, ≥ 8 mm** (user-touchable) |
| AD1 / AD2 isolated measurements | HT_GND / ISO_GND side | ISO1 / ISO2 | reinforced (part of the domains above) |

**Consequences of D6 (reinforced primary ↔ secondary), to work through in phases 1–3:**
- Spacing: the working voltage across the primary ↔ ISO_GND barrier is up to ≈ DC+ + 420 V peak
  (the DAB bridges swing both sides); take the reinforced creepage / clearance for that working
  voltage, PD2, MG IIIa from IEC 62368-1 (expect well above 5.1's ≈ 6.4 mm) and use it for every
  barrier on L1 / L6, the slots if needed, and the minimum distance through insulation (prepreg) for
  the inner-layer plane splits (reinforced: ≥ 0.4 mm solid insulation or a rated thin-sheet build).
- Isolator parts must carry a reinforced rating: ISO1 / ISO2 Si8661 – confirm the fitted grade
  (Si8661**BD** = 5 kVrms reinforced, SOIC-16W); the UART Si8621BD is reinforced; DRV5 / DRV6
  (Si8275BB, 2.5 kVrms basic) do not cross the primary ↔ secondary barrier (both sides of each are
  in one domain) – keep it that way.
- Transformers: TR3 (two-chamber EC42: divider + 3 tape layers over each winding, core counted as
  primary) and TR1 (EEL19 margin-tape design: 3 + 3 mm margins, 4-layer barriers) must be checked
  against the reinforced creepage / distance-through-insulation for that working voltage – the
  EEL19 6 mm margin creepage was sized for 264 Vac mains, possibly not enough for the DC+-referenced
  primary; **TR2 (heater, EF25) has not been studied at all** – it needs a reinforced construction
  too (new item).

Tasks:
1. Fork: `kicad_next/` with a new project file, `tools/next/` (copies of the sheet scripts,
   `gen_symbols.py`, the footprint map) and `lib/tube_psu_next.pretty` (already holds the EEL19 and
   EC42 footprints) in its fp-lib-table.
2. **Stackup (6 layers, JLC 1.6 mm, e.g. JLC06161H-3313 as on the control card):**
   L1 components + power copper, L2 GND planes (one per domain), L3 signals / HV routing,
   L4 power distribution (DC+, 15V, +3V3 pours), L5 GND planes, L6 bottom components + signals.
   Planes split at every isolation barrier on all six layers; no plane crosses a barrier.
3. Design rules: JLC 6-layer capabilities in a `.kicad_dru` (track / space / via / annular ring),
   net classes per domain and voltage (HV, primary, secondary, signal), KiCad creepage and clearance
   rules between domains from the table above, keep-outs under transformers and at the barriers.

## Phase 4 – simulations of the 5.2 circuits

- Measurement models (`simulation/`): add the 5.2 networks (bias buffer, VAC/DC_LINK fixes, INA241A
  or LMH6643 channels with the ESL RC and a shunt-L model, AUX / 5V followers) and rerun AC + mux
  leakage; every channel < 0.5 LSB at the planned dwell (NR 4c).
- **New DAB ngspice model**: both half-bridges (STF7N60M2 Coss, dead time), the EC42 transformer
  (leakage + Lm from `simulation/dab`), split caps; check ZVS vs dead time and gap, phase shift and
  rms currents at 400 W, both directions.
- Flyback: rerun the EEL19 design with the iW1819 parameters (Rsense, Vsense) if D4 = iW1819.
- Memory rule: ≤ 8 parallel ngspice runs, 3 GB RAM in total.

## Phase 5 – parts placement (Fable)

A **Fable** session places the parts on the 6-layer board; Claude prepares the inputs and reviews.
Inputs handed to Fable: the 5.2 netlist / schematic, the board outline with fixed items (mounting
holes, J2, heatsinks – D3), the stackup and rules from phase 1, the domain table, and these
placement constraints:

1. Isolation: one continuous barrier per domain; transformers (TR1, TR2, TR3), DRV5/DRV6, ISO1/ISO2,
   the UART isolator and the measurement isolators straddle their barrier; nothing else crosses it.
2. Power loops first: PFC switch / diode / bulk-cap loops, DAB half-bridges with their split caps
   (C47/C54 at the TR3 primary pins, C16/C55 at the secondary pins), flyback switch / clamp loop –
   smallest area, on L1 with the return on L2.
3. Gate drivers next to their MOSFETs (short gate / source-return loops); bootstrap parts at the driver.
4. Shunts with Kelvin sense pairs to the current amplifiers; the ESL RC at the amplifier; amplifiers
   close to J2; each J2 analog output's 100 Ω / 100 pF at the J2 pin; measurement section away from
   the switch nodes and transformers.
5. Thermal: the DAB TO-220 row on one straight line along a new bent-plate heatsink (its hole
   positions come out of the placement – deliver a bracket drawing), clear of the control card;
   Q10/Q11 (LLC) anywhere / SMD (NR 5.4); tall parts (electrolytics, transformers 29–32 mm) checked
   against the enclosure height.
6. TR3 over the old TR3 + L3 area, axis along x (NR 2.3); TR1 near its 5.1 position (NR 1.3);
   UART header at the board edge on its island; fly-lead holes per the EEL19 footprint.
7. Assembly: SMD on L1 where possible (JLC single-side assembly is cheaper), bottom side only for
   small passives; JLC part orientation / fiducials / tooling holes.

Deliverable: a placed board (no routing), a placement report with a screenshot per region, the
barrier distances and the list of constraint trade-offs. Claude reviews it against the constraints
before routing.

## Phase 6 – routing and planes

- Planes per the stackup (L2 / L5 GND per domain, L4 power pours), then the HV and power paths
  (width from current, clearance from the rules), then signals (Kelvin pairs, gate drives, SPI to the
  isolated ADCs, J2 analog). Routing approach chosen after the placement review (Claude / Fable with
  KiCadRoutingTools, or the user by hand).
- Current check of the power paths (PFC, DAB, heater) against D5.

## Phase 7 – verification and release

- ERC, DRC (JLC rules, creepage / clearance per domain), schematic parity, unconnected = 0.
- Simulations of phase 4 rerun on the final values.
- Board plots / 3D renders (`.github/workflows/board-plots.yml` – add the 5.2 board), height check.
- JLC outputs: gerbers + drill (6 layers, JLC06161H stackup), BOM + CPL from `lib/jlc_parts.csv`
  (`tools/jlc_bom.py`), rotation check of the CPL in JLC's viewer.
- Bring-up list: what to measure first (transformer Lm / Lleak, flyback rails at idle and 12 W, DAB
  ZVS at no load and phase shift at 400 W, ADC channel leakage, UART isolation test).
